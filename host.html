        Your invitation link

        Send this one link to everyone, in a group chat or one by one. Each person who replies gets their own guest pass and a private link to check details or change their RSVP later. Their reply appears in Guests and you get notified.

        Share on WhatsApp

        The exact address stays hidden until someone confirms. Each phone number or email can only reply once, so the same person can’t RSVP twice.

        Optional: personal links

        Want a few people to get a link that greets them by name before they reply? Add them in Guests and send their own link. You don’t need this if you’re sending the one link to everyone.

        Nudge pending guests

        Only for guests you added in Guests who haven’t replied. Email reminders are sent from your Gmail to those with an email address (once a day at most). For phone numbers, tap WhatsApp to send a ready-written note.

          Polaroid photos

          Easiest: open the invitation (View & edit ↗) while signed in here and tap Edit. You can change photos, captions, the notes on the back of each photo and all the card wording right on the page.

          The four Polaroids on the invitation. Swap the illustrations for your own photos (flowers, the Airbnb, drinks, candles). Captions are handwritten on the frame. Photos are resized on your phone before upload so the invitation stays fast.

            Link preview artwork

            The picture WhatsApp shows when you share a link (cropped to 1200×630). WhatsApp caches previews, so changes can take a while to appear on links already sent.

            Background music

            Optional. Plays softly when a guest opens the envelope, with a pause button. Paste a direct link to an MP3 file (it must start with https:// and end in .mp3). Only use music you have the rights to share.
